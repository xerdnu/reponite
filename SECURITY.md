# Security Policy

Found a security vulnerability in [Reponite](https://reponite.com)? Please report it
**privately** — do **not** open a public issue or discussion.

## How to report

- Email **security@netblast.se** with the details, **or**
- Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new).

Please include steps to reproduce and, where possible, the impact. We'll acknowledge
your report within a couple of days and keep you updated on the fix.

## While you're testing

Use **your own** account and your own app. Reponite is full of other people's
unreleased work and their feedback, so please don't access, alter or collect
anything belonging to another user, and don't run tests that would degrade the
service for them. If you land on someone else's data by accident, stop, and tell
us what you saw rather than how much of it you could get.

Findings that need no account and touch nobody else — headers, TLS, a page you can
reach signed out — are always fine to test.

Thanks for helping keep Reponite and its users safe.
