# Reponite — Feedback & Support

The place to **request features, ask questions, and report bugs** for
[Reponite](https://reponite.com) — where indie developers get their apps tested by
other developers, and earn the testing they need by testing.

## Where to go

- 💡 **Idea or feature request** → [Discussions → Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas) — and upvote the ones you want.
- ❓ **Questions and help** → [Discussions → Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a)
- 💬 **Anything else** → [Discussions → General](../../discussions/categories/general)
- 🐛 **Bug report** → [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose)

Feedback on **someone's app** belongs on Reponite itself, in the campaign you
joined — not here. This repo is about Reponite the site.

## ⚠️ Please don't post private info here — this repo is public

Never paste:
- Closed-testing opt-in links, TestFlight invites, or any URL to a build you have not released
- Login details you hand out to testers, or an API key from your own product
- Screenshots showing your account, your testers' email addresses, or someone else's unreleased app

For anything tied to your account, use the private channels below.

## 📧 Private support

- **Account or sign-in problems, credits, anything about your own data** → **hello@netblast.se**
- **Abuse, spam, or a user who should not be here** → **abuse@netblast.se**
- **Privacy, your personal data, deletion requests** → **privacy@netblast.se**
- **Security vulnerability** → **security@netblast.se** (or GitHub's [private reporting](../../security/advisories/new)) — please **don't** open a public issue for security.

We usually reply within a day.
